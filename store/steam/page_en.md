## About this game

**Aim, read the wind, fire.** Frontier Tank is a turn-based pixel art artillery game made to play with friends. Every shot is a matter of angle and power against the wind, on ground that breaks apart with each explosion.

**Online battles from 1 vs 1 to 4 vs 4.** Walk into the Game Hall, set up a room with your team and find rivals of your level. Matches run in lockstep with the server: your shot leaves with exactly the aim and power you released.

**Twelve weapons with their own specials.** From the Bricklayer that shatters into pieces to the Lightning Rod that calls down three bolts, every weapon has its own angles, projectile and a POW special that changes how you play. The three Super True weapons only drop from bosses.

**Co-op dungeons.** Four dungeons with three stages each, with bosses that summon minions, freeze heroes and fly across the map. Maps from level 1 to 16, with threats and rewards, set the difficulty and what you bring home.

**Your gear, your way.** Items with random bonuses, strengthening up to +12, crafting currencies to reroll, add and remove bonuses, and an auction house between players using in-game currencies.

**Free to play, never pay to win.** The shop only sells looks: nothing bought with money gives attributes, and there are no paid loot boxes.

### Features

- Turn-based artillery with wind, destructible terrain and a trail of your last shot
- Online PvP from 1v1 to 4v4 with matchmaking by level
- Four co-op dungeons with bosses and maps from level 1 to 16
- 12 weapons, each with its own POW, and auras that grow with strengthening
- Random bonuses, a Blacksmith, crafting currencies and a player auction house
- Chat with a word filter and reporting, plus an offline mode against the AI
- English and Brazilian Portuguese

## Early Access

**Why Early Access?** Combat, dungeons, the economy and the auction house are in place; we want to tune the balance, the economy and the servers with real players before 1.0.

**How long?** About 6 to 12 months.

**What will change before the full version?** More dungeons and bosses, bids and currency exchange in the auction house, guilds, pets and quests, new battle maps and a cosmetics-only season pass.

**What is the current state?** Complete online PvP and dungeons, 12 weapons, 4 three-stage dungeons, 12 battle maps, the auction house and mail, English and Portuguese.

**Will the price change?** No: the game is and stays free to play. The shop only sells looks.

**How will the community be involved?** Through the Steam forums, in-game reports and suggestions, and closed tests of new features.
