## 3. What we collect and why

**Account.** Your account name and password, which we only keep as an irreversible hash (PBKDF2-SHA256 with its own salt). Also the creation date, your last login and the version of these texts you accepted, with the date. Why: to log you in, protect your account and prove your acceptance. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b); LGPD art. 7, V) and the exercise of legal rights (LGPD art. 7, VI).

**Sessions.** A hash of your session code and its expiry (30 days). Why: to keep you logged in. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

**Character and progress.** Character name, looks, level, experience, items, in-game currencies, maps and what is in your Mail. Why: the game itself. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

**Activity log.** Purchases in the in-game shop, Blacksmith actions, coupons, rewards, matches, Auction listings and purchases, Mail letters and the messages you write in the public chat. Why: security, fraud and cheating prevention, handling complaints and moderation. Legal basis: legitimate interests (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); LGPD art. 7, IX).

**Chat reports.** Who reported, who was reported, the reason, the reported message and the messages around it. Why: chat moderation. Legal basis: legitimate interests and enforcing the Terms of Use.

**Access logs.** Your IP address and the date and time when you create your account or log in. Why: the Brazilian Internet Civil Framework (art. 15) requires us to keep these records for 6 months, and to protect against attacks. The IP address is also used, only in the server's memory and for a few minutes, to limit login attempts. Legal basis: legal obligation (GDPR art. 6(1)(c); LGPD art. 7, II) and legitimate interests.

**Presence.** Which game server you are connected to. Why: to stop the same account from playing on two servers at once. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

**Steam.** When you play through Steam: your SteamID and the purchases made with your Steam Wallet (order number, item, amount, currency and status). Why: logging in with Steam, delivering purchases, refunds and fraud prevention. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legal obligation.

**We do not collect** your real name, tax number, e-mail (unless you write to us), phone number, address, card details, precise location, contacts or sensitive data.

## 4. Data on your device

The game writes files in its data folder (or in the browser's storage, in the web version): offline progress, language, sound options, the server address and, if you tick "Remember", your session code. We do not use cookies. You delete these files by uninstalling the game or clearing the site's data in your browser.

## 5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data. It is only shared:

- **with other players**: your character name, level, looks, public chat messages, the seller name on Auction listings and what happens in matches;
- **with the providers that run the game for us** (server and database hosting), under contract and only for that purpose;
- **with Valve (Steam)**, when you play through Steam: login and purchases go through Steam, under Valve's privacy policy;
- **with authorities**, when there is a court order or a legal obligation.

## 6. International transfers

The servers may be outside Brazil or the European Union (for example, in the United States). In that case we use the safeguards of the LGPD (art. 33) and of the GDPR (standard contractual clauses).

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account, character, items and Mail | while the account exists |
| Sessions | 30 days, or until you log out |
| Chat messages in the activity log | 90 days |
| Rest of the activity log | 365 days |
| Chat reports | 180 days after review |
| Access logs (IP, date and time) | 6 months (Brazilian Internet Civil Framework, art. 15) |
| Steam purchases | 5 years (tax and consumer protection obligations) |

**When you delete your account**, we immediately erase the account, character, items, sessions, Mail, active listings, your chat messages and your character name in the logs. The rest of the activity log is no longer linked to you and is erased at the end of its period. Access logs and purchases are kept for the period the law requires and then erased.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to: confirmation that we process your data and access to it; rectification; anonymisation, restriction or erasure of unnecessary data; data portability; erasure; information about who we share it with; withdrawing consent; objecting to processing based on legitimate interests; and review of decisions made solely by automated means (we make no such decisions with effects on you; the chat word filter only hides forbidden words).

**How to exercise them:**

- **Help → My account → Download my data**: a JSON file with everything we keep about your account.
- **Help → My account → Delete account**: deletes the account and its data, with your password.
- For any other request, or if you cannot log in: {{EMAIL}}. We answer within 15 days (and always within one month).

You can also complain to the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD, www.gov.br/anpd) or, in the European Union, to the data protection authority of your country.

## 9. Children and teenagers

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. From 13 to 17 (or below the age of digital consent of your country, in the European Union), a parent or guardian must agree to these texts. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, it will be deleted. Parents and guardians can ask for access to the data or for the deletion of the account at the e-mail above. The game has no paid loot boxes and uses nobody's data for advertising.

## 10. Security

Passwords stored with PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 rounds) and their own salt; session codes stored only as a SHA-256 hash; encrypted connections (HTTPS and WSS) on public servers; restricted access to the database; limited login attempts. If a security incident may cause relevant risk or harm, we will notify the authorities and the people affected (LGPD art. 48; GDPR arts. 33 and 34).
